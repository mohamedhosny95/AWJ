# Third-party exercise media

`data/exercise-media.json` records every exercise source and individual final asset version. The app serves approved media locally without runtime stock APIs, third-party video players or athlete endorsement claims.

## Pexels

- Push-up footage: Ketut Subiyanto, https://www.pexels.com/video/man-doing-push-ups-4804794/
- Easy-jog footage: RDNE Stock project, https://www.pexels.com/video/man-warming-up-on-a-soccer-field-7187117/
- Licence rechecked 8 October 2026: https://www.pexels.com/license/

Pexels permits free use and modification, including app use. Its restrictions include implied endorsement, offensive depictions, selling unaltered copies, redistribution on stock/wallpaper platforms and use as a trademark. The app's instructional excerpts preserve the person and movement without suggesting sponsorship.

## free-exercise-db

Forty exercises use individually mapped photographs from https://github.com/yuhonas/free-exercise-db. Source directories and raw image URLs are included per exercise in the inventory. The project's README expressly supports local image use: https://github.com/yuhonas/free-exercise-db/blob/main/README.md. Its declared licence is the Unlicense: https://github.com/yuhonas/free-exercise-db/blob/main/LICENSE.md.

## Existing project references

Thirty-six existing illustrated references remain static and explicitly labelled. The corrected Dead Hang reference has image-generation provenance recorded from this audit. Original creator/licence evidence for the other existing references was not present in the repository. They are recorded as replacement-needed, rather than asserted to be licensed real-trainer media. These are retained interim assets, not newly sourced footage.

Three earlier illustrations (Hip Thrust Machine, Resistance Band Pulldown and Cable Pull-Through) were rejected for incorrect equipment or movement and removed from display. Their source records and rejection reasons remain in the inventory.

The supplied chest-supported-row reference image is a layout reference only and is not distributed with the app.
